3

Label every protein

S2Recorded offline run

Each protein gets two labels: how likely it is to be reachable by antibody (where it sits in the cell) and whether it resembles a known virulence factor.

What this shows

  • 21 of 40 proteins have an Unknown location, and they stay Unknown. Folding them into "not surface" would silently drop candidates.
  • 16 of 40 proteins are virulence-associated; that is why a virulence flag sends a protein to review, not to the bin (fHbp and NadA are real antigens).

What it does not show

  • Localisation here is a stand-in read from UniProt text, not PSORTb, because PSORTb was not installed.

Predict what the immune system would see

S5Real-tools run

T-cell epitopes are the short peptides presented on HLA molecules; B-cell epitopes are the surface patches an antibody could bind. Both are predicted along each protein.

What this shows

  • 858 class I epitopes predicted for this protein over a 27-allele HLA panel; 102 are presented by many alleles.
  • 20 of the 36 predicted linear B-cell regions are shaded; the rest are shorter or weaker.

What it does not show

  • Class II (NetMHCIIpan) and conformational B-cell (DiscoTope-3.0) predictions did not run. MHCflurry is an open-source alternative to NetMHCpan, which has a licence we do not hold.
  • A predicted epitope is a hypothesis. Whether an animal recognises it is what the animal study in step 12 is for.

Design decision. B-cell prediction uses BepiPred-3.0, not 2.0 (AUC 0.574, close to random). Sequences over 1,023 residues are chunked with overlap so the end of a long protein is not lost.

Write the protocol, and how it could be wrong

S8Recorded offline run

Each candidate gets a structured protocol with controls, who does each step, and one required line: the result that would prove the prediction wrong.

Protocol for IgA-specific serine endopeptidase: 13 steps, widths proportional to the estimated duration. Robot does 3, robot does part of 8, a person does 2.

  1. s01Order codon-optimised gene for IgA-specific serine endopeptidase7 to 21 d
  2. s02Clone into His-tagged expression vector1 to 3 d
  3. s03Transform expression host1 to 2 d
  4. s04Colony PCR to confirm insert0.5 to 1 d
  5. s05Small-scale expression and induction1 to 2 d
  6. s06Lyse cells; clarify lysate0.5 to 1 d
  7. s07Ni-NTA (His-tag) purification1 to 2 d
  8. s08Endotoxin removal0.5 to 1 d
  9. s09Quantify residual endotoxin (LAL)0.5 to 1 d
  10. s10THP-1 culture and PMA differentiation3 to 5 d
  11. s11Stimulate differentiated THP-1 with purified antigen1 to 2 d
  12. s12ELISA readout1 to 2 d
  13. s13RT QPCR readout1 to 2 d

robot does it robot does part a person does it

Falsification condition (required). At the in-vitro stage this candidate is falsified if EITHER (a) it cannot be expressed soluble and purified to the endotoxin limit, OR (b) the cytokine/transcript response does not exceed the endotoxin-matched LPS control and the polymyxin B-treated arm. A response explained by residual endotoxin is not evidence for the antigen.

What this cascade does not measure

  • adaptive immunity
  • antibody titre
  • neutralisation
  • T-cell memory
  • protection
  • population HLA diversity (THP-1 is a single-genotype cell line)

What this shows

  • Endotoxin removal and an LPS control come before the THP-1 assay, and the protocol validator enforces the order: residual endotoxin is the dominant false-positive source in this cascade.

What it does not show

  • Durations are coarse engineering estimates and cost is not estimated. The cascade measures innate immune activation only: nothing here says anything about antibody, memory or protection.

What this shows

Design the study that could refute it

S10Recorded offline run

A ranked list is a set of hypotheses. The animal study is the first experiment that can refute them, so the design maps each prediction to the assay that would kill it.

18 prediction rows 3 testable 15 not testable yet 17 inputs still need a person.

Every prediction is paired with the readout that can refute it
Upstream predictionAnimal readoutFalsified if
S3 antigenicity rankAntigen-specific IgG ELISA titreNo titre rise over the adjuvant-only arm
S5 T-cell epitopesIFN-γ ELISpot against predicted peptide poolsResponse to the whole antigen but not to the predicted pools: antigen right, epitope prediction wrong
S5 B-cell epitopesCompetition ELISA or peptide-array mappingSera bind the antigen but not the predicted epitopes
S6 population coveragePanel of HLA-transgenic linesResponse absent in backgrounds predicted to be covered
S7 structureBinding to folded vs denatured antigenSera recognise only the denatured form: the conformational epitope was an artifact
Functional protectionSpecies-appropriate functional assayTitres rise but functional activity is absent

What this shows

  • In this offline run only 3 of 18 rows are testable, because the tools that make the upstream epitope and coverage predictions were not installed: nothing yet to refute.

What it does not show

  • It is a design ready for ethics review, never results. Mouse immunogenicity has no reliable link to human protection.

Design decision. HLA-transgenic mice, because wild-type mouse MHC does not present the epitopes predicted for humans: without them S5 and S6 stay unfalsifiable.
